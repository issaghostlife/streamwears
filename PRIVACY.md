# Streamwears Privacy

Streamwears is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection.

## Information Streamwears May Access

Depending on which features you choose to use, Streamwears may request access to device capabilities such as:

- Camera
- Microphone
- Local network or internet connectivity
- Connected streaming-service authorization
- Compatible wearable-device connections

These permissions are used to provide app functionality such as livestreaming, camera capture, audio capture, account connection, and supported smart-glasses features.

## Third-Party Services

When you choose to connect a supported third-party service, that service may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Examples may include streaming platforms and compatible wearable-device services.

Streamwears does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

## Credentials and Tokens

Do not post access tokens, OAuth credentials, stream keys, passwords, API keys, or other sensitive credentials in public GitHub issues.
