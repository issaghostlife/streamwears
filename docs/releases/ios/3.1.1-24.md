# Streamwears 3.1.1 (24)

[Back to release notes](../README.md) · [Get Streamwears on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/Ee36dYF9)

Streamwears iOS 3.1.1 (24) is now available to external TestFlight testers.

- More reliable glasses preview during longer streams.
- Broadcasts remain active while glasses video recovers.
- Clearer Disconnect Protect status while reconnecting.

Please try a longer live stream with your compatible Meta glasses and send feedback on camera preview, reconnecting and broadcast continuity.

Before testing, enable **Developer Mode** in Meta AI and confirm the **Device Access Toolkit** is installed on your glasses. Then open Streamwears, tap **Start Preview**, and approve camera access when prompted.

Send feedback in Streamwears or through [support](https://streamwearsirl.com/support). TestFlight shows which builds are currently available to your account.
