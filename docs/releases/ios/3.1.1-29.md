# Streamwear 3.1.1 (29)

[Back to release notes](../README.md) · [Get Streamwear on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/Ee36dYF9)

Build 29 has been uploaded to TestFlight and is processing.

- Preview can return to a glasses session that worked previously after you stop and restart it.
- The microphone selector shows connected external audio inputs by their device name.

Please try with compatible Meta glasses: enable **Developer Mode** in Meta AI, install or update the **Device Access Toolkit** on the glasses, then tap **Start Preview** in Streamwears. Try restarting preview and choosing a Bluetooth microphone paired to your iPhone by its displayed name. Send feedback about preview, microphone audio and reconnection through Streamwears or [support](https://streamwearsirl.com/support).
