# Streamwears 3.1.1 (22)

[Back to release notes](../README.md) · [Get Streamwears on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/Ee36dYF9)

- Choose between linked Instagram accounts after signing in.
- A simpler account selector with profile photos and Streamwears branding.
- Clearer sign-in verification, reconnecting and account-switch recovery.
- Find My Gear improvements.

Please try your compatible Meta glasses with Streamwears and let us know how camera preview and going live work for you.

1. In Meta AI, enable **Developer Mode** and confirm the **Device Access Toolkit** is installed on your glasses.
2. Open Streamwears and tap **Start Preview**.
3. Approve camera access when prompted, then check your preview before going live.

Please also test Instagram comments, Find My Gear and reconnecting your accounts. Send feedback in Streamwears or through [support](https://streamwearsirl.com/support).

TestFlight controls which builds are currently available to each tester. See the [published update](https://github.com/issaghostlife/streamwears/releases/tag/ios-3.1.1-22-instagram-gear) for the original announcement.
