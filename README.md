## Go live from your iPhone

Streamwears is available on the **App Store**. Broadcast from your iPhone’s front or rear camera to **up to three enabled destinations at the same time, for free**. Optional Full Access unlocks unlimited simultaneous destinations.

This page covers the **iPhone-camera App Store edition**. Start with your phone and your streaming accounts; no extra camera hardware is required.

## Your broadcast, in one place

- **Front or rear camera:** preview your picture before you go live.
- **Portrait or landscape:** choose the format for your broadcast.
- **Camera and audio controls:** adjust video quality, frame rate and microphone selection.
- **Destination controls:** see which accounts are connected and choose where each live goes.
- **Live chat and status:** follow supported platform conversations and keep track of your broadcast.
- **Connection recovery:** see clear connection and reconnect status as Wi-Fi or cellular conditions change.

## Your first live

1. [Download Streamwears from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6815077840).
2. Open **Destinations** and connect your streaming accounts.
3. Choose up to three destinations for your free broadcast and set your stream title.
4. In **Studio**, choose your iPhone camera, allow camera and microphone access, and tap **Start preview**.
5. Check your picture and sound, then tap **Go live**. Tap **End live** when you finish.

Requires iOS 17.2 or later. Destination availability, chat and other platform features depend on the account’s eligibility, permissions and live-streaming access. You can review available purchases in the App Store or in Streamwears.
